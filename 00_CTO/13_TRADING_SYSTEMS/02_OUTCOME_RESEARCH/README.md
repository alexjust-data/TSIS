# 02_OUTCOME_RESEARCH

Estado: design surface.

## Purpose

Define how TSIS measures what happens after events.

Outcome Research studies consequences of market phenomena, not trading
decisions.

## Inputs

- `event_table`
- audited price and liquidity data;
- calendar;
- corporate actions and halt constraints.

## Outputs

- `outcome_table` schema proposal;
- outcome horizons;
- continuation, reversal, failure, volatility, liquidity and risk metrics;
- evidence rules for promoting or demoting event definitions.

## No-goals

No entries, stops, targets, sizing or PnL claims belong here.
