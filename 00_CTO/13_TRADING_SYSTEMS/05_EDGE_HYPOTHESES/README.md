# 05_EDGE_HYPOTHESES

Estado: design surface.

## Purpose

Capture why an edge might exist before it is converted into a strategy or model.

## Inputs

- Event Library;
- Market Science;
- Sersan distillation when promoted;
- Outcome Research evidence;
- empirical anomalies.

## Outputs

- causal or structural hypotheses;
- falsification criteria;
- evidence requirements;
- links to events and outcomes.

## No-goals

No complete strategy, PnL claim or model score is authority here.
