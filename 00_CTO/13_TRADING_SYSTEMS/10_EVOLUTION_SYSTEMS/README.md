# 10_EVOLUTION_SYSTEMS

Estado: design surface.

## Purpose

Define where AlphaEvolve/OpenEvolve-style systems may propose candidates after
the lab has governed evaluators.

## Inputs

- audited data;
- event tables;
- outcome tables;
- locked evaluators;
- constraints;
- lineage requirements;
- OOS validation policy.

## Outputs

- candidate proposal protocols;
- mutation/search boundaries;
- fitness contracts;
- promotion gates;
- archive requirements.

## No-goals

Evolution Systems must not modify evaluators, data scope or labels after seeing
results.
