# TSIS Phenomenon Discovery

## The Theory of Scientific Knowledge Discovery in Financial Markets

Version: 1.0 (Draft)

Status: Constitutional Research Architecture

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

TSIS Market Representation Architecture defines **how the market is
represented**.

TSIS Phenomenon Discovery defines **how scientific knowledge is
extracted from those representations**.

The distinction is fundamental.

The Representation Architecture ends with a valid, temporally legal and
reproducible **State**.

Phenomenon Discovery begins with that State.

``` text
Reality
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Primitive
    ↓
Feature
    ↓
Representation
    ↓
State
========================
Phenomenon Discovery
========================
    ↓
Research Question
    ↓
Hypothesis
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Phenomenon
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Strategy
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Fundamental Principle

TSIS does not attempt to discover trading strategies directly.

TSIS attempts to discover **observable market phenomena**.

Strategies are merely one possible application of validated knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# What is a Phenomenon?

A phenomenon is **not**:

-   a feature,
-   a state,
-   a strategy,
-   a prediction,
-   an outcome.

A phenomenon is a **scientific explanation** proposed to describe a
recurring pattern observed across many market states.

Examples:

``` text
Liquidity Vacuum

Absorption

Momentum Ignition

Auction Imbalance

Price Discovery

Exhaustion

Volatility Compression
```

These concepts do not exist as raw observations.

They emerge through research.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Constitutional Statement

Representations belong to Data Foundation.

Phenomena belong to Research.

Knowledge belongs to Scientific Validation.

Strategies consume validated knowledge.

This document establishes the constitutional architecture governing
every future discovery process inside TSIS.
