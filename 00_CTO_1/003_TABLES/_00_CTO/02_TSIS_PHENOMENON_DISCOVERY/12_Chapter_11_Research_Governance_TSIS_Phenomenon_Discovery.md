# 11. Research Governance

## 11.1 Purpose

Research Governance defines how scientific investigations are initiated,
conducted, reviewed, promoted and archived inside TSIS.

Its objective is to ensure that knowledge evolves through evidence
rather than intuition, authority or isolated profitable backtests.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.2 Fundamental Principle

Every research activity shall be governed by explicit, reproducible and
versioned protocols.

Research without governance is exploration.

Governed research becomes institutional knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.3 Governance hierarchy

``` text
Research Question
        ↓
Research Protocol
        ↓
Hypothesis
        ↓
Evidence Collection
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Knowledge Review
        ↓
Registry Promotion
```

Every stage must preserve lineage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.4 Research protocol

Every investigation shall define:

``` text
research_id
question_id
population_id
control_population_id
state_version
feature_registry_version
validation_protocol
success_criteria
failure_criteria
```

The protocol must exist before analysing results.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.5 Independence

Research governance remains independent of:

-   implementation,
-   strategy profitability,
-   machine learning model,
-   optimisation algorithm,
-   researcher preference.

Scientific conclusions arise from evidence only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.6 Promotion criteria

A knowledge object may be promoted only after demonstrating:

-   reproducibility,
-   empirical support,
-   documented scope,
-   explicit limitations,
-   successful review,
-   complete provenance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.7 Review

Every canonical promotion should include review of:

``` text
Question validity
Population definition
Evidence quality
Statistical methodology
Alternative explanations
Known limitations
```

Review strengthens knowledge rather than slowing research.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.8 Research archive

All research artefacts shall remain archived.

Examples:

``` text
Rejected hypotheses
Negative evidence
Exploratory analyses
Deprecated knowledge
```

Historical research remains part of institutional memory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.9 Metadata

Every governed research project shall expose:

``` text
research_id
owner
creation_date
status
protocol_version
knowledge_outputs
evidence_sets
review_history
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.10 Constitutional rule

Scientific governance is mandatory for every canonical knowledge object.

Within TSIS, authority derives from reproducible evidence governed by
documented research protocols rather than from profitable historical
examples or subjective judgement.
