# 15. Autonomous Scientific Discovery

## 15.1 Purpose

The objective of TSIS is not only to assist human research but to
progressively automate the scientific discovery process while preserving
reproducibility, governance and scientific integrity.

Autonomy does not replace the scientific method.

Autonomy executes the scientific method at scale.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.2 Fundamental Principle

Every autonomous research agent shall operate inside the constitutional
boundaries defined by TSIS.

Autonomous systems may:

-   propose questions,
-   generate hypotheses,
-   build candidate features,
-   define research populations,
-   execute experiments,
-   summarize evidence.

They shall never promote canonical knowledge without the governance
process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.3 Human--AI relationship

``` text
Human
    defines constitutional architecture
            ↓
AI Research Agents
    execute governed discovery
            ↓
Evidence
            ↓
Human + Governance
            ↓
Canonical Knowledge
```

The architecture remains human-governed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.4 Autonomous capabilities

Future research agents may autonomously:

``` text
Detect anomalies
Discover recurring states
Generate hypotheses
Suggest feature families
Generate control populations
Design validation experiments
Identify contradictory evidence
Prioritize research backlog
```

Every action must remain reproducible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.5 Research contracts

Every autonomous investigation shall begin from an explicit research
contract.

Minimum contents:

``` text
research_question
population
control_population
representations
feature_registry_version
state_version
validation_protocol
stopping_criteria
```

No autonomous experiment shall run without a governed contract.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.6 Scientific constraints

Autonomous systems shall never:

-   redefine ontology,
-   change feature semantics,
-   modify registry entries,
-   alter state definitions,
-   rewrite canonical knowledge.

These actions require governance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.7 Evidence-first autonomy

Autonomous systems are forbidden from optimizing directly for
profitability.

The optimization sequence remains:

``` text
Better Representation
        ↓
Better Questions
        ↓
Better Evidence
        ↓
Better Knowledge
        ↓
Potentially Better Strategies
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.8 Continuous learning

Every completed investigation enriches Research Memory.

Future agents should consume:

-   prior questions,
-   rejected hypotheses,
-   validated knowledge,
-   contradictory evidence,
-   research backlog.

Autonomy is cumulative rather than episodic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.9 Agent lineage

Every autonomous action shall declare:

``` text
agent_id
agent_version
research_contract
inputs
outputs
execution_time
builder_versions
knowledge_dependencies
```

Complete provenance is mandatory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15.10 Constitutional rule

Autonomous research systems are scientific assistants rather than
scientific authorities.

Within TSIS, canonical knowledge is always established through governed
evidence, reproducible validation and architectural review, regardless
of whether the investigation was initiated by a human researcher or an
autonomous agent.
