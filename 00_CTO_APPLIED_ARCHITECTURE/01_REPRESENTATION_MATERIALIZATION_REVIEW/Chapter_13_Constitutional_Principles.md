# Chapter 13 --- Constitutional Principles

## Objective

This chapter establishes the constitutional principles governing the
relationship between Canonical Market Representations, their Physical
Representations and every institutional implementation within TSIS.

These principles are technology-independent.

They shall remain valid regardless of future architectural or
engineering evolution.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 1 --- Semantic Authority

The semantic authority of every implementation originates exclusively
from its Canonical Market Representation.

Implementations shall never redefine canonical meaning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 2 --- Architecture Before Engineering

Architectural decisions precede engineering decisions.

Engineering implements architecture.

It does not create architecture.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 3 --- Representation Before Implementation

No Physical Representation shall exist without an approved Canonical
Market Representation or an explicitly governed institutional purpose.

Implementation never justifies representation.

Representation justifies implementation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 4 --- Explicit Justification

Every Canonical Market Representation and every enabling institutional
artifact shall possess an explicit institutional justification.

No institutional asset shall exist solely because it is technically
convenient.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 5 --- Governance Before Materialization

Before implementation begins, the Physical Representation shall be
governed through explicit institutional contracts.

Builders, schemas and validators execute governance.

They do not replace it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 6 --- Certification Before Promotion

Institutional promotion requires objective certification evidence.

Successful implementation alone is insufficient.

Promotion shall never bypass certification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 7 --- Traceability

Every institutional implementation shall preserve complete architectural
traceability from the Epistemological Architecture to the deployed
implementation.

Institutional trust depends upon reconstructable lineage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 8 --- Technology Independence

Canonical Market Representations are independent of implementation
technology.

Storage engines, programming languages and infrastructure may evolve.

Canonical meaning shall remain stable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 9 --- Separation of Responsibilities

The architectural layers of TSIS shall remain independent.

The Epistemological Architecture defines knowledge.

The Market Representation Architecture defines Canonical Market
Representations.

This framework governs materialization.

Engineering governs implementation.

No layer shall assume responsibilities belonging to another.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principle 10 --- Institutional Evolution

Implementations may evolve, be replaced or retired.

Canonical Market Representations remain the stable semantic reference
unless explicitly revised by the Market Representation Architecture.

Implementation evolution shall not silently modify institutional
meaning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Constitutional Summary

The complete institutional flow is:

``` text
Epistemological Architecture
        ↓
Market Representation Architecture
        ↓
Canonical Market Representation
        ↓
Representation Justification
        ↓
Materialization Decision
        ↓
Physical Representation
        ↓
Institutional Contracts
        ↓
Physical Realization
        ↓
Institutional Certification
        ↓
Institutional Promotion
        ↓
Institutional Asset
```

Every institutional asset within TSIS shall be explainable, reproducible
and traceable through this constitutional chain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Closing Statement

The purpose of this framework is not to create more documentation.

Its purpose is to ensure that every institutional implementation within
TSIS remains a faithful realization of the scientific architecture from
which it originates.

Scientific knowledge defines meaning.

Architecture governs realization.

Engineering preserves that meaning through implementation.

Together, they form a single institutional system whose integrity is
maintained through explicit governance, reproducibility and
traceability.
