## Matrix Conclusion

The book fills a real architectural gap only if it becomes a bridge from semantic representation authority to existing physical-contract machinery. Its strongest unique contributions are:

1. `representation_justification_record`.
2. `materialization_decision_record`.
3. `canonical_to_physical_mapping`.
4. `architectural_traceability_record`.
5. explicit classification of `Enabling Institutional Artifact`.

The book should not become a second source of truth for:

1. canonical market ontology;
2. materialization modes already defined in Market Representation Chapter 16;
3. table creation process;
4. dataset schemas;
5. validators;
6. promotion authority.
